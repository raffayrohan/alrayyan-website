What exactly do you get?

Cheap fencing fails at two points: columns without bases that move at the first wind or impact, and slack mesh that waves and rusts from the bottom where it touches soil and moisture. Our cure is the same across all our work: concrete bases for every main column, column spacing calculated by fence height and the area's wind strength, and galvanised mesh tensioned in stages with top and bottom tension wires that keep it straight for years.

We build fencing for residential and commercial land to define and protect boundaries, farm and rest-house fencing with vehicle and pedestrian gates, and court and school fencing at greater heights with thicker mesh. Fencing is officially registered among our Google Business categories (Fence contractor), and is built by the same steel team that has built our shades and structures since 2014.

Mesh fence, iron fence, or privacy screen? Mesh is the economical solution for defining boundaries and protecting land with open visibility — ideal for land, farms and courts. If you want privacy with the boundary, our iron screens have their own page. If you want higher security for a sensitive site, we combine: outer mesh + a reinforced iron gate, or a taller fence in thicker wire. At inspection we walk the land's boundaries with you and propose by its nature, length and your budget — without defaulting you to the most expensive.

An important boundary point: before installing any fence on land, confirm your boundaries from your deed and plan — and install exactly on the boundary line, not inside it (losing metres) nor outside it (encroaching on a neighbour). When in doubt we advise a surveyor to fix the corners before we pour a single base; a concrete base in the wrong place costs more to remove than the surveyor's fee.

And for farms: leave a gate wide enough for your equipment and transport vehicles, and a fence height that accounts for jumping animals if you keep livestock — details we ask you about at inspection because changing them after execution is always costly.

How is the price calculated?

Fence pricing is calculated from length first, then height, wire gauge, gate count and ground condition (level, or needing levelling and base digging in rocky soil). So we give no per-metre price by phone; inspection inside Jeddah is free: we walk the boundaries, fix corners and gates, then an itemised written quote — mesh, columns, bases and gates — fixed once you sign, however long the project.

Pricing factors

  • Total fence length and corner count
  • Height and wire gauge (land / farms / courts)
  • Gate count and widths (vehicle / pedestrian)
  • Ground condition: levelling and base digging in normal or rocky soil
  • Extra bracing for wind-exposed sites
