What exactly do you get?

A pergola in Jeddah is not a seasonal decor piece: it is an outdoor structure living under a sun that exceeds 45°C and coastal humidity that eats ordinary paint. So we build our pergolas the way we build our shades: galvanised steel first, oven-baked powder coating second, and fixing on calculated bases — not garden tiles. The difference shows after three years: our pergola keeps its colour and tension, while an ordinarily painted one peels and rusts from the screws.

We build modern pergolas with clean lines, CNC laser-cut pergolas with patterns you choose from our samples or your own design, and cladding and WPC wood-alternative pergolas for those who want wood's warmth without its maintenance — with clear or tinted Lexan covers that protect your seating from rain and soften glare without blocking light. Every pergola is drawn to your site's measurements: no ready-made sizes forced onto your garden.

Who is this service for?

This service fits you if you have a garden, rooftop or courtyard you want turned into seating that is actually used, not a photo posted once: family evenings, winter guests, and morning coffee in the shade. We also build pergolas for cafes, restaurants and rest houses to commercial specifications. At inspection we study the sun's path over your site almost hour by hour — because a Jeddah pergola shading the afternoon is designed opposite to a morning one.

Fixing on calculated bases

Especially for wind-exposed rooftop pergolas — safety before looks.

Iron, wood alternative, or laser-cut? Painted modern iron lasts longest and is easiest to maintain, suiting modern facades. Laser-cut adds privacy and ornament, softening glare with patterned shade — ideal for seating close to neighbours. WPC gives warm wood feel for resorts and rest houses without yearly painting, but costs more as a material. Many clients combine: painted iron frame + WPC slats + tinted Lexan roof — at inspection we help you build a combination that fits your budget and your site's sun direction.

A point many miss: a rooftop pergola in Jeddah faces winds many times stronger than a garden one, especially on high floors in Obhur and Al-Hamdaniyah. Our rooftop pergolas are therefore calculated with reinforced bases and columns and a considered height that neither blocks a neighbour's view nor breaches building rules — ask us at inspection about permitted height limits before dreaming of a two-storey pergola.

And lighting is not a last-minute add-on with us: spot and strip wiring runs inside the frame during fabrication, so you see not a single hanging cable after installation. If you plan a ceiling fan or outdoor speakers later, tell us at inspection and we will leave ready conduits inside the columns.

How is the price calculated?

A pergola's price is set by its area and material: a painted iron frame alone, or with WPC slats, laser cutting, a Lexan roof and hidden lighting — each addition has its own clear line. So we do not price from a WhatsApp photo; the inspection is free, where we measure and draw column and lighting layout, then you receive an itemised written quote whose lines you can remove or add before signing, and the price is fixed once you approve it.

Pricing factors

  • Pergola area, height and column count
  • Material: painted iron / CNC laser-cut / WPC slats and cladding
  • Lexan roof, clear or tinted, or no roof
  • Hidden lighting, spots and electrical fittings
  • Installation place: ground garden or rooftop (wind-reinforced bases)

Questions before you book

Will the pergola withstand Jeddah's sun and humidity?

Yes: a galvanised frame with powder coating, and Lexan and WPC are inherently water- and humidity-resistant — covered by our official 10-year warranty against manufacturing and installation defects and colour change.

Can the pergola be fully rain-covered?

Yes, with a Lexan polycarbonate roof sloped for rain drainage — clear to keep the light, or tinted to soften heat — decided at inspection according to your seating direction.

How long does a medium garden pergola take?

After design and measurements are approved, fabrication in the workshop then installation within a few working days — the exact schedule is written in the quote (laser cutting and lighting extend fabrication slightly).

Do you build pergolas on villa rooftops?

Yes, with reinforced bases and columns calculated for rooftop winds and a considered height. We check the roof's insulation condition at inspection so no fixing harms it.

Can I choose the laser-cut pattern myself?

Yes: ready patterns with us, or your own design drawn and approved before cutting — pieces are powder coated after cutting so no edge stays bare.

How do I book an inspection?

Call 0550044696 or WhatsApp a photo of the spot with its approximate size — we book the free inspection and draw your pergola to your site's measurements.
