What exactly do you get?

A tensile shade is not 'a bigger shade'; it is an engineered structure: a fabric membrane tensioned at calculated forces on columns and cables, taking its conical, pyramidal and wave forms from the tension itself. Any error in tension calculation or column angles shows up as wrinkles, water pooling and wind flutter. So our projects start with an engineering drawing: dimensions, heights, tension angles and wind loads — and the client approves the drawing and the materials, with their origin certificates, before the first piece is cut.

Our fabric is German PVDF at 1050g with a treated surface that resists Jeddah's dust grime and washes with water, fully HF-welded — no stitching, no holes. In this system we have built conical school shades, facility parking, mosque yards and courts — their photos are in our gallery. Engineer Fawaz Al-Qahtani supervises column locations and installation method on site, as in all our large projects.

PVDF or PVC for wide areas? Korean PVC 1100g is excellent for home parking and limited spans, but on wide wind-exposed areas PVDF wins: higher tensile strength, a grime-resistant surface, and longer life under permanent tension. The rule: the larger the area, the higher the structure and the greater the project value, the more you lean to PVDF. If your area is medium and your budget is counted, PVC on a stronger frame may serve you — we present both options with a drawing and price for each, and you decide on a fair comparison.

The most dangerous mistake in tensile projects: columns planted where their place 'looks' right rather than where the drawing calculates them. A column half a metre off changes every tension angle and creates a water pocket no later tensioning fixes. So column and base locations are set by surveyed measurement before bases are poured, personally supervised by Engineer Fawaz — as one of our customers noted in his review: personal supervision of column locations, installation method and area.

And for schools and mosques specifically: our drawing accounts for student and worshipper movement paths and peak times, and we propose execution phases in holidays and breaks so the facility's operation is not disrupted — the execution schedule is part of the quote, not a later surprise.

How is the price calculated?

Tensile projects are priced from their drawing, not area alone: membrane type and weight, column heights and wind loads, number and size of concrete bases, and the required form. So our first step is a site inspection and measurement (free inside Jeddah), then an engineering drawing with an itemised quote: membrane, structure, bases and installation. For facilities and entities: we work with contracts, material approvals and progress-linked payment stages.

Pricing factors

  • Shaded area, structure height and form (conical/pyramidal/wave)
  • Membrane type: German PVDF 1050g and its tensile value
  • Column and cable count and concrete base sizes
  • Ground condition: new bases or fixing to existing structure
  • Entity requirements: drawings, material approvals and phased schedule

Questions before you book

What is the difference between a PVDF shade and an ordinary PVC shade?

PVDF is an architectural membrane with tensile strength up to 4500 N/5cm, a grime-resistant surface and longer life under permanent tension — made for wide areas and projects. PVC is excellent for home parking and smaller spans.

Do you build school and mosque shades in Jeddah?

Yes — conical and pyramidal shades for yards and parking, with drawings and specifications suitable for approval, and execution phases respecting the facility's operation and holidays.

Do you issue drawings and material approvals before execution?

Yes: an engineering drawing with dimensions, heights and tension angles, and materials named with origin certificates — approved by you in writing before fabrication begins.

How long does a tensile project take?

Set by the drawing and project size and fixed in the contract: structure and membrane fabrication in the workshop, base pouring, then installation and final tensioning — school projects we usually schedule in holidays.

Does the warranty cover membrane and structure?

Yes — the official 10-year warranty certificate covers manufacturing and installation defects and colour change for membrane and structure together.
