What exactly do you get?

Our sandwich panel work is not 'sheet metal on steel': it is a complete light building system — insulated panels with an injected polyurethane core at 40 kg/m³ density between two painted galvanised sheets, mounted on a galvanised steel frame fixed to engineered concrete bases. That density is exactly the difference between a cool, quiet room and one that whistles in the wind and leaks heat at every joint; that is why density and thickness are named in writing in your quote, and the panels' origin certificates are handed over with the contract.

In Jeddah we build rooftop rooms and villa annexes, driver and maid rooms, site offices, and commercial warehouses and hangars — from measurements to interior finishing and key handover. Every project starts with a free inspection where we set the right thickness for your use: we will not sell you 10 cm if 5 cm is enough for a driver's room, and we will not fit 5 cm on a permanent residential annex and leave you paying the difference in electricity every month.

Which thickness should you choose? For a driver's room or storage with limited AC hours, 5 cm is enough and saves you the price difference. For a permanent residential annex or a rooftop bedroom, we recommend 7.5 cm minimum, and 10 cm for west-facing walls and roofs exposed to sun all day — the price difference comes back from electricity bills within about two summers. For large warehouses and hangars, thickness is calculated with wind direction and roof height. Our rule, stated plainly: thickness is bought once; electricity is paid every month.

Why do some cheap ready-made rooms creak and pop? Because their panels are low-density and their frames are screwed straight onto roof tiles with no bases, so the metal expands and contracts in Jeddah's heat and moves with every breeze. Our fix is simple engineering: concrete bases that lift and anchor the frame, calculated expansion joints, and evenly torqued fixing screws — which is why we can sign a 10-year warranty while others offer one year on 'installation only'.

One last point nobody mentions: the room's door direction and AC position are decided at inspection, not on installation day. A door facing seasonal dust winds will fill the room with dust however good the insulation is, and an AC mounted on a panel wall without a bracket will vibrate and drone. We sketch a simple room plan with you — door, windows, AC and power points — before the first panel is cut.

How is the price calculated?

We do not publish a ready per-metre price, because the real cost of sandwich panel work changes with your project: panel thickness and density, room area and height, roof condition and the need for concrete bases, and the number of doors, windows and interior finishes. Any 'per-metre' figure you see without an inspection is a marketing number that will change on execution day. With us, inspection and measurements are free, after which you receive an itemised written quote — panels, frame, bases and finishing each on its own line — and the price is fixed once you sign.

Pricing factors

  • Panel thickness (5 / 7.5 / 10 cm) and 40 kg/m³ injection density
  • Room area, ceiling height and number of rooms
  • Roof condition: new concrete bases or fixing to an existing base
  • Insulated doors and windows and double glazing
  • Interior finishing: floors, electrics, plumbing and paint

Questions before you book

How long does a sandwich panel room take in Jeddah?

A standard room is installed within 24–48 hours once the concrete bases are ready, because panels are cut to size in the workshop. Larger projects such as warehouses are scheduled in writing in the quote according to area.

Is the inspection and measurement really free?

Yes — inspection inside Jeddah is free with no obligation: we take measurements, set the right thickness, and send a written quote via WhatsApp.

What is the difference between 40 density and cheaper panels?

Cheaper panels are lower density: they compress over time, insulate less, and creak in wind and heat. 40 kg/m³ density means stable insulation and lasting stiffness — the difference shows in your electricity bill and the room's lifespan.

Does the warranty cover panel colour change?

Yes — the official 10-year warranty certificate covers manufacturing and installation defects and colour change, and you receive it in writing at handover.
