What exactly do you get?

Our difference from a traditional 'tent hirer' is that we are installation contractors at origin: the tents and shades we set up for your event are installed to the same standards as our permanent projects — columns, bases and wind-calculated fixing, clean fabrics stored and inspected before every occasion, and an installation team that knows your event date does not move. We set up wedding and family-event tents, corporate and school event shades, and celebration and seasonal coverings in Jeddah and its outskirts.

We also build permanent tents and majlis in the bayt shaar system for those wanting fixed setup for a rest house or events venue — and the difference between temporary and permanent setup we discuss honestly: if your occasion is once, temporary setup is cheaper; if your events recur, permanent installation recovers its value quickly.

Temporary setup or permanent installation? For a single occasion or one season, temporary setup is the right economic decision however tempting permanent looks. For a rest house rented weekly or a permanent events venue, permanent installation in the bayt shaar system or architectural shades recovers its value within a season or two and saves repeated booking effort. Tell us your expected yearly event count and we calculate it with you in numbers before you decide.

The most important question for any outdoor event in Jeddah: what if a strong wind blows on the event night? Our answer is procedural, not reassuring words: calculated fixing from the start, a tension and fixing check before handover to you, and a direct contact point with our team throughout your event for emergencies. Cheap tents are set up with surface pegs and ropes — and the difference only shows on the night that cannot afford error.

And a booking tip: fix your date and approximate guest count even if details are not finalised; early booking locks your date in our schedule, and size and colour details are approved later at a measurement visit to the event site.

How is the price calculated?

We publish no ready setup prices because your event's cost is built from tent or cover size, guest count, setup duration, event location and the fixing provisions its surface requires. Any instant phone price will change on seeing the site. On contact: give the date, guest count and location; we visit the event site to measure (inside Jeddah), then you receive an itemised written offer — setup, installation and removal — and your price and date are fixed by booking.

Pricing factors

  • Tent/cover size and guest count
  • Setup type: fabric tents or PVDF architectural shades
  • Setup duration (one day or several days)
  • Event location and its surface: concrete, soil or grass
  • Booking timing: busy seasons book early
