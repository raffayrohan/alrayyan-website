What exactly do you get?

Our bayt shaar is a complete outdoor building, not 'fabric on poles': the box system means walls and roof as a closed insulated box — five layers between outside and inside covering thermal and waterproof insulation and interior finishing — so your majlis sits as comfortably in August as in January. The frame is galvanised steel with powder coating, and the fronts follow your taste: heritage Sadu in its known colours, secured glass opening your majlis onto the garden, or a combination.

We fabricate to your land's size and your guest count: small family majlis, or large hospitality majlis with an independent entrance and its facilities, with electrics, lighting, AC and interior decor — Sadu, curtains and flooring. Every tent is a bespoke project drawn before the first piece is cut — which is why we sell no 'ready sizes' and give no price before inspection.

Royal or regular? Our 'regular' tent is not a synonym for poor: it is the same box system and insulation with simpler finishes and more fabric fronts. Royal adds secured glass, luxury finishes, full Sadu and greater heights. If the majlis is for family most of the time, a fully insulated regular tent serves you and saves a respectable difference; if it is your hospitality face to the world, royal is an investment in impression. At inspection we help you see where the difference genuinely shows and where it is not worth paying for.

A question nobody asks before execution: where does winter rain go off the tent's roof? Tent roofs are near-flat, and a wrong slope turns the roof into a pond leaking at the first joint. We build a calculated slope with drainage directed away from the entrance and glass fronts, with joints sealed in engineering silicone — details whose value shows in the first winter, not on handover day.

And advice from Jeddah majlis experience: do not build the majlis at the maximum size your land allows. Leave a perimeter for service and ventilation and a shaded path between house and majlis; a majlis two metres smaller with a comfortable perimeter gets used more than a giant one suffocating the courtyard. We draw the layout on the ground with you before any fabrication.

How is the price calculated?

A bayt shaar is a fully bespoke project, so its price is built from its size, system and finishes: majlis dimensions and height, front type (Sadu or secured glass), interior finishing level, AC and lighting, and floor and base preparation. Any ready price for a tent without inspection is a guess that will change. Our inspection is free: we visit your land, draw the layout, and present an itemised written quote — frame, insulation, fronts and finishing — from which you choose your level with full awareness.

Pricing factors

  • Majlis dimensions, height and seating count
  • Front type: heritage Sadu / secured glass / mix
  • Interior finish level: Sadu, curtains, flooring and lighting
  • AC and electrics and number of points
  • Floor and concrete base preparation if needed

Questions before you book

Is a bayt shaar usable in Jeddah's summer?

Yes — with the box system, five-layer insulation and AC sized to the majlis area: this is exactly what separates our tents from fabric tents that become hot storage in summer.

What is the difference between royal and regular with you?

The same frame and insulation; the difference is fronts (secured glass and full Sadu in royal), finishing level and height. At inspection we show you the difference line by line with its price.

How long does a bayt shaar take to fabricate and install?

Set by size and finishes and written in the quote: fabrication in the workshop first, then installation and finishing at your site — large majlis with glass and AC need a longer schedule than regular ones.

Does the warranty cover insulation and leaks?

The official 10-year warranty certificate covers manufacturing and installation defects and colour change, and our insulation, slope and drainage execution is part of that commitment — any leak note from a workmanship fault is treated under warranty.

Do you build majlis for rest houses and farms outside residential districts?

Yes — we build in Jeddah's rest houses and farms and its outskirts; state your location when booking the free inspection.
