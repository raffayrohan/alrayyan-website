What exactly do you get?

What separates a shade that lives 10 years from one that sags after two summers is two things you cannot see in photos: the fabric's real weight and how it is welded. Ordinary stitching pierces the fabric with hundreds of holes that leak water and tear at the edges in wind; we weld our fabrics with high-frequency (HF) technology that fuses the layers into one piece with no holes, at tensile strengths of 2800–4500 N/5cm depending on fabric type — figures we write in your quote together with the fabric name, its country of origin and its certificate.

We build every style Jeddah asks for: cantilever shades with no front columns for villa parking, conical and pyramidal shades for schools and facilities, tensile shades for wide areas, and pool, walkway and seating shades. The frame is always galvanised steel with oven-baked powder coating that resists Jeddah's air rust, fixed on engineered concrete bases — never surface screws into courtyard tiles.

Who is this service for?

This service fits you if you are tired of a market shade that sagged or tore; if you are building new villa parking and want comfortable entry with no front columns; or if you run a school or facility needing parking and yard shades with contract-grade specifications. For large projects we work to drawings with material approval before execution, and Engineer Fawaz Al-Qahtani personally supervises column locations and the installation method — as our customers testify in their Google reviews.

Which fabric should you choose? For everyday villa parking, Korean PVC 1100g gives the best value: heavy, taut, and cheaper than PVDF. For wide parking, projects and wind-exposed facades, German PVDF is worth its difference: longer life and a surface that resists dust grime. French fabrics we recommend for seating and shades close to the eye, where looks and colour matter. Light woven HDPE fabrics suit temporary shading and very tight budgets — and we say it plainly: they do not compare with PVC and PVDF lifespan, and we will not sell them to you as an equal alternative.

A small detail that makes a big difference in Jeddah: the shade's slope and rain drainage direction. A perfectly flat shade pools water and dust in its middle until it bows, and a shade sloped toward the villa door pours winter rain on everyone entering. At inspection we set the slope away from entrances and walkways, and calculate the low edge height so it neither blocks the driver's view nor collides with an open car door — measurements drawn before the first steel piece is cut.

And for villa owners specifically: cantilever is not always the answer. If your parking is very wide or the soil at the edge is weak, two well-placed side columns can be stronger and cheaper than an over-extended cantilever. At the free inspection we tell you which style genuinely serves your parking — even if it is the cheaper one.

How is the price calculated?

A car shade price is not settled by a single per-metre number: it changes with fabric type and weight (PVC, PVDF or French), shade area and car count, frame style (cantilever is more engineering-intensive than a column shade), and the need for new concrete bases. That is why our inspection is free: we measure at your site, show you fabric samples with their certificates, then you receive an itemised written quote — fabric, frame, bases and installation as separate lines — and the price does not change after you sign.

Questions before you book

What is the best fabric for a car shade in Jeddah?

For home parking: Korean PVC 1100g for its stable tension and excellent value. For wide areas and projects: German PVDF 1050g for its longer life. At inspection we show you samples and origin certificates so you choose knowingly.

Is a cantilever shade safe with no front columns?

Yes, when engineered properly: a cantilever arm on a galvanised frame with concrete bases designed for its load. We have built cantilevers at large spans — including 12-metre awnings personally supervised by Engineer Fawaz, as our customers' reviews describe.

How long does a car shade take to fabricate and install?

After measurements and fabric are approved, frame and fabric are fabricated in the workshop, then installed at your site within a few working days depending on car count — the schedule is fixed in the quote.

Does the warranty cover both fabric and frame?

Yes — the official 10-year warranty certificate covers manufacturing and installation defects and colour change for the fabric and the powder-coated frame.

Do you install shades for schools and facilities?

Yes — conical, pyramidal and tensile shades for schools, mosques, facilities and commercial parking, with specifications and drawings suitable for contract approval.
