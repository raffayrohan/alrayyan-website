What exactly do you get?

Lexan is the common trade name for polycarbonate sheets, and its real strength is that it does not break: it withstands impacts, hail and maintenance loads, unlike acrylic which cracks and fibreglass which yellows and crumbles. But the market has thin imitation 'Lexan' sheets that yellow within two years and bow in the heat; the difference from the genuine is sheet thickness and the UV protection layer — so we name thickness and origin in your quote, and install on galvanised powder-coated frames with calculated expansion joints, because polycarbonate expands and contracts in Jeddah's heat more than metal does.

In Lexan we build villa and shop entrance shades, pergola and seating roofs, walkway and pool shades, and courtyard screens that block sight while passing light. Who is this service for?

This service fits you if you want cover that keeps natural light: a house entrance that floods at every rain, an exposed walkway between villa and annex, a pergola you want roofed without darkening the seating, or a courtyard whose privacy you want without turning it into a dark cave. Lexan is also lighter on existing structures than glass — a practical choice when modifying an existing building.

Clear or tinted? Clear gives maximum light and suits north entrances and inner walkways, but passes more heat. Tinted (bronze or grey) softens glare and heat and suits seating, pools and sunny facades. And Lexan or glass? Glass is heavier, costlier and needs a stronger frame; Lexan is lighter, unbreakable and faster to install — for entrances, pergolas and walkways we usually recommend Lexan, while large glass facades are a different discussion we have at inspection.

The most common complaint we hear about previous Lexan installations: 'the sheet creaks every noon.' Its cause is a sheet screwed tight with no expansion joints, so at noon it expands and rubs the frame. Our fix is simple and calculated: fixing holes wider than the screw by a studied margin, expansion joints at edges, and closure strips keeping dust and water from between sheet and frame. Details you do not see — but whose absence you hear daily if neglected.

We clean and hand over the sheets with their protective film removed in front of you at handover, so you receive a surface free of transport and installation scratches — and we show you its correct cleaning with water and mild soap only, because strong detergents are polycarbonate's first enemy.

Questions before you book

How much is a Lexan shade?

Our Lexan shades start from SAR 180 as published on our Google profile; the final price depends on thickness, area and frame — settled in a written quote after a free inspection.

Does Lexan break or yellow over time?

Genuine polycarbonate with a UV layer does not break or yellow early; yellowing is the mark of imitation sheets with no protective layer. We name origin and thickness in your contract, with our 10-year warranty on installation and frame.

Should I choose clear or tinted?

For entrances and low-sun walkways: clear. For seating, pools and sunny facades: tinted softens heat and glare. We show you the grades in person at inspection.

Is Lexan good as a pergola roof?

It is one of its best uses: light, unbreakable, and light-passing. We install it with drainage slope and calculated expansion joints so it neither creaks nor leaks.

How long does an entrance Lexan shade take?

Small entrances and walkways are usually completed within a few days of approving size and colour — frame fabrication in the workshop, then fast clean installation at your site.
