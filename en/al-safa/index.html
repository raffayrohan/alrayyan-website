We know this district and build in it

Al-Safa's urban character mixes family villas, residential buildings and shops on main streets, and many of its homes have stood for years needing exterior renewal more than new building: a parking shade consumed by the sun, a courtyard screen rusted under ordinary paint, or an old rooftop room with a plain metal roof. Our speciality in the district is this controlled renewal: careful removal of the old, inspection of what lies beneath, and new installation to a specification lasting ten years, not two seasons.

Al-Safa's closeness to our Al-Hamdaniyah base makes our schedule in the district flexible: fast inspections, and installations split across days suiting occupied homes — we work, clean and leave without turning your house into an open workshop. For the district's buildings and shops we build collective parking shades, Lexan entrances and shop pergola fronts to commercial specifications on schedules that do not obstruct business.

Most repeated in Al-Safa requests: cantilever shades for front parking because the district's inner streets cannot bear extra columns obstructing movement, slat screens for facing villas, and sandwich panel rooftop rooms at full residential insulation thickness — because an Al-Safa room is usually a son's room or a guest room, not storage.
