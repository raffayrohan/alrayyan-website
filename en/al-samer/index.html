We know this district and build in it

Most Al-Samer homes are medium family villas and houses with front parking exposed to sun nearly all day, which is why car shades top the district's requests — specifically Korean PVC 1100g, the heaviest home-parking fabric and the most tension-stable in Jeddah's heat. The common mistake we see in the district: light market shades installed years ago that sagged until they pool winter rain; our cure is not re-tensioning them but replacing fabric and frame to a correct specification once.

The district's second request: sandwich panel rooms and annexes for families their homes have grown too small for — a son's room, an extra majlis, or a rooftop guest room. In Al-Samer we install at residential thicknesses (7.5 and 10 cm) because these rooms are genuinely lived in, and we calculate the sun's direction on your roof with you: a west-facing room needs extra thickness and insulation however small it is.

And because the district is a close-knit family area, most of our work there comes by neighbour recommendation — one house installs a shade and three houses on the same street follow. We keep that recommendation with what actually preserves it: a written price that does not change between one neighbour and the next for the same specification, clean on-time execution, and a 10-year warranty certificate you can show your neighbour before he asks for our number.

Questions before you book

What fabric suits my Al-Samer home parking?

For exposed home parking: Korean PVC 1100g — the heaviest in its class and the most tension-stable, HF-welded with no stitching. We show you its sample and origin certificate at the free inspection.

Does a rooftop room always need 10 cm thickness?

Not always: it depends on the room's direction and use. West-facing and permanent residential rooms we advise at 10 cm; service rooms at 5 or 7.5 cm — we calculate it with you at inspection rather than defaulting you to the dearest.
