Shades & Pergolas in North & South Obhur In Materials That Withstand Sea Salt

Obhur — both its northern and southern parts — is the harshest environment for any steel and fabric in Jeddah: marine humidity and airborne salt that eat ordinary paints from the screws within two seasons. So everything we install in Obhur starts from a fixed coastal specification: galvanising first, oven-baked powder coating second, and genuine named fabrics — with a 10-year warranty covering colour change and installation defects.

We know this district and build in it

Obhur housing has two dominant characters: luxury villas and palaces in North Obhur with wide parking and large outdoor seating, and resorts, rest houses and holiday homes in South Obhur around beaches and marinas. Demand here leans to the highest specification: PVDF tensile shades for large parking, seating pergolas with Lexan roofs overlooking the sea, and tents and majlis for rest houses — with special sensitivity to looks, because Obhur facades are built to be seen.

Obhur's first enemy is not the sun but salt: sea spray droplets dry on the frame leaving salt crystals that pit any ordinary spray paint, and on light fabric accelerate its stiffening and cracking. That is why we refuse market shortcuts in Obhur: no ungalvanised steel, no paint without oven baking, no fabric without an origin certificate and a written weight. We advise rest-house owners especially to wash their structures with fresh water periodically — a simple maintenance tip we write for you at handover with its schedule.

Obhur's open sea winds raise fixing calculations a full grade: deeper bases, thicker columns at large spans, and studied shade slope so shades do not face the prevailing wind like a sail. At inspection we read your site's position from the sea: a first row on the corniche is not a third inner row — and the specification follows the reading, not a unified price.
