We know this district and build in it

Al-Hamdaniyah is one of north Jeddah's largest and fastest-growing residential districts: medium and large family villas, relatively wide inner streets, and nonstop demand for rooftop annexes and driver rooms in sandwich panel and for front-yard parking shades. The district's family character means most of our projects here are purely residential: a two-car cantilever shade that does not hide the villa facade, a rooftop room insulated at 7.5 or 10 cm because it will be a real bedroom, and a slat screen blocking the neighbour's eye without suffocating the courtyard.

And because we are in the district itself, we know its practical details: which streets take the strongest north winds, which directions catch afternoon sun on parking, and where soil is sandy and needs deeper bases. This local knowledge enters every inspection: shade slope direction, room panel thickness, screen height — decisions built on your Al-Hamdaniyah location specifically, not a generic template.

After-installation service in Al-Hamdaniyah is our strongest by proximity: any warranty note — fabric tensioning, a door adjustment, an insulation joint check — reaches our team in the shortest possible time without waiting for a distant visit schedule. Many of the district's customers came to us by neighbour-to-neighbour recommendation, and most of the Google reviews you read on our site are from north Jeddah owners.

Rooftop sandwich panel rooms and annexes — at residential 7.5 and 10 cm thicknesses

Cantilever car shades for front parking with no columns blocking entry
